Common questions

How is pricing determined?

Every engagement is scoped based on the number of agents, integrations required, and the complexity of your workflows. We provide a fixed-fee proposal after a free discovery session — no surprises.

Do I need to replace my existing systems?

No. Our agents plug into your existing stack — Sage, Xero, HubSpot, WhatsApp, and more. We add intelligence without disruption.

What's included in on-site FDE support?

Our Field Deployment Engineers are local experts who work alongside your team during deployment. They ensure the agent works in your context, speaks your language, and fits your culture.

How quickly can I see results?

Pilot engagements are designed to prove measurable KPIs within 4–8 weeks. Most clients see tangible ROI (time saved, errors eliminated) within the first two weeks of go-live.

Is there a minimum commitment?

Our Pilot tier is designed to be low-risk. After the pilot, you decide whether and how fast to scale — there's no obligation to expand.
